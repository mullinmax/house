# Ladder laser cut kit (SendCutSend)

Flat DXFs written by `tools/ladder_drawings/export_mono_kit.py`, in inches.
Cut lines on layer 0, bend lines dashed on layer BEND.
Quantity is in each file name. Material: A36 hot rolled pickled and oiled steel, no finish (it gets cold blued),
1/4 in except the balcony corner plates (3/8 in) and the 3/16 in tread brackets.
Round 6 (11/24/2026): tread bracket corners rounded; one head clip pair per stringer top, the same part on both flights
(qty 2 of each hand); the post saddle now has cheeks up the stringer and tabs to the landing beam, replacing the foot clips
and the lower head angles. Base plate clip holes are countersunk from the bottom for flat head screws; nothing is tapped.
Round 7 (11/24/2026): tread bracket webs are a smooth hull 1 in round their stringer bolts (the foot bracket keeps its round 6 shape),
tread strips have rounded bottom end corners, and every free outline corner is rounded (corners at bend lines stay as drawn).
The knee braces get gusset plates (knee_gusset_*) instead of clip angles, so the clip angles drop from 30 to 22.
Round 8 (11/24/2026): the lower flight's foot bracket is gone. A floor shoe (floor_shoe_qty1: base under the stringer's level foot,
two cheeks up outside the first bracket's webs on its lower bolt, one slab anchor through each end tab) takes the foot, so every
tread has the same bracket (11 per hand). Balcony ledger gets a 4th bolt beside the head beam; drill the ledger holes tight.
Maxwell chose the NO STRIP option (11/24/2026): this folder is that kit (1 3/4 in oak treads, no tread strips, brackets with no front
flange and a 2 1/2 in seat with 3 x 11/32 holes for 5/16 x 1 1/2 lags). The older strip version is in ../sendcutsend_strips/.
Cost pass (11/24/2026): tread brackets are 3/16 in A36 (solid FEA 10 to 12 ksi away from corners, about half of 0.6 Fy); bend
deduction taken as 0.30 in for them, check the seat flange in SendCutSend's preview. Everything else stays 1/4 or 3/8.
Round 10 (11/24/2026): standard parts wherever possible. The laser cut base plates are gone: every slab post stands between
two clip angles cut from stock L 4x4x3/8 (8 in long, 6 pieces, buy as angle, not in this kit), 2 grade 5 3/8 through bolts each,
2 slab anchors each. Floor shoe is now a base plus one wall bent up 118 deg onto the stringer's sloped top at the toe, with its own
3/8 through bolt and 3 anchors. Saddle tab runs the full cheek height with rounded corners. Tread bracket front bottom corner is
R 2, riser corner R 1, and the seat ends where the round starts. South knee post is mitred at its foot; the foot gusset is a
rounded triangle on that line. The balcony deck grows to the rail lines (north and west); the old L plates are replaced by
balcony_corner_plate_qty2 (SW and NW, mirror pair: bend one each way), whose flange bolts up through the end beam.
The NE guard post runs down as the north knee post (one tube), so the north frame has only top and foot gussets.
Round 11 (11/24/2026): the post bases go back to a small square plate (Maxwell: the stock angle bases looked overbuilt).
post_base_plate_qty3 (1/2 in, 7 x 7, corners R 0.75) is one part under all three slab posts: four wedge anchors at the corners, and two
