# Ladder laser cut kit (SendCutSend)

Flat DXFs written by `tools/ladder_drawings/export_mono_kit.py`, in inches.
Cut lines on layer 0, bend lines dashed on layer BEND.
Quantity is in each file name. Material: A36 hot rolled pickled and oiled steel, no finish (it gets cold blued),
1/4 in except the base plates and balcony L plates (3/8 in).
Round 6 (11/24/2026): tread bracket corners rounded; one head clip pair per stringer top, the same part on both flights
(qty 2 of each hand); the post saddle now has cheeks up the stringer and tabs to the landing beam, replacing the foot clips
and the lower head angles. Base plate clip holes are countersunk from the bottom for flat head screws; nothing is tapped.
Round 7 (11/24/2026): tread bracket webs are a smooth hull 1 in round their stringer bolts (the foot bracket keeps its round 6 shape),
tread strips have rounded bottom end corners, and every free outline corner is rounded (corners at bend lines stay as drawn).
The knee braces get gusset plates (knee_gusset_*) instead of clip angles, so the clip angles drop from 30 to 22.
Round 8 (11/24/2026): the lower flight's foot bracket is gone. A floor shoe (floor_shoe_qty1: base under the stringer's level foot,
two cheeks up outside the first bracket's webs on its lower bolt, one slab anchor through each end tab) takes the foot, so every
tread has the same bracket (11 per hand). Balcony ledger gets a 4th bolt beside the head beam; drill the ledger holes tight.
The no strip tread option (thicker 1 3/4 in oak, no tread strips, longer seat tabs with 5/16 lags) is in ../sendcutsend_no_strip/.
